Safe storage tips

Mind the danger zone

Bacteria multiply fastest between 5 °C and 60 °C.

Hot or cold, not in between

Keep cooked chicken at 60 °C or above, or at 5 °C or below.

Cool rice quickly

Cool cooked rice quickly and refrigerate it within an hour or two.

Reheating is not a fix

Reheating will not remove toxins that some bacteria, such as B. cereus, can make.
